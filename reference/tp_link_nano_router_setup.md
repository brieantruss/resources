**INSTRUCTIONS:**

Based on the label on your **TP-Link TL-WR802N V4**, here is a step-by-step guide to get it up and running.  
The most common ways to use this nano router are **Standard Wireless Router Mode** (to share a wired internet connection wirelessly) or **Repeater/Range Extender Mode** (to boost an existing Wi-Fi signal).

## **Step 1: Power and Connect the Hardware**

1. **Power the device:** Plug the micro-USB power cable into the router and connect it to a wall outlet or a powered USB port (it requires $5\\text{V} \= 1\\text{A}$).  
2. **Connect to Internet (if using Router/AP mode):** Plug an Ethernet cable from your main modem or internet wall jack into the Ethernet port of the nano router.  
   * *Note: If you are setting it up as a Range Extender/Repeater, leave the Ethernet port disconnected for now.*

## **Step 2: Connect to the Nano Router's Wi-Fi**

On your computer, smartphone, or tablet, open your Wi-Fi settings and look for the default network listed on your device's label:

* **SSID (Network Name):** TP-Link\_6424  
* **Wireless Password / PIN:** 49047943

## **Step 3: Access the Management Page**

1. Open a web browser (like Chrome, Safari, or Firefox).  
2. In the address bar, type: **http://tplinkwifi.net** (if that doesn't load, try typing **192.168.0.1**).  
3. Create a **Login Password** when prompted. This is a secure password you'll use to log into this settings page in the future (keep it distinct from your Wi-Fi password).

## **Step 4: Run the Quick Setup Wizard**

Once logged in, the **Quick Setup** wizard will guide you through the rest:

1. Click **Next** to begin.  
2. **Select your Operation Mode** depending on what you need:  
   * **Wireless Router:** Standard mode. Connects to a modem and shares internet with your wireless devices.  
   * **Repeater/Range Extender:** Connects to your existing home Wi-Fi to extend its range.  
   * **Access Point (AP):** Connects to an existing router via Ethernet to add Wi-Fi capability to that network.  
3. Follow the prompts based on your choice:  
   * For **Router Mode**, select your WAN connection type (usually **Dynamic IP** for standard home setups).  
   * For **Repeater Mode**, select your main home Wi-Fi network from the list and enter your home network's Wi-Fi password.  
4. **Personalize your settings:** You will be prompted to change the wireless network name (SSID) and set a new, secure Wi-Fi password.  
5. Click **Finish** or **Save** to complete the setup. The router will reboot to apply the changes.

💡 **Tip:** If you ever get locked out or need to start fresh, find the tiny **Reset** hole on the side of the device while it is powered on. Use a paperclip to press and hold it down for about 5 seconds until the LED light flashes rapidly, then release it. This restores the default factory settings shown on your sticker.
