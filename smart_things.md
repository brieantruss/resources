# Adding a device from WiZ

To integrate your WiZ lights with SmartThings, you'll need to enable the SmartThings integration within the WiZ app and then connect it in the SmartThings app. First, in the WiZ app, navigate to Settings, then Integrations, and toggle on SmartThings. You'll receive a 5-minute code. Next, in the SmartThings app, go to Add Device, find WiZ (under All Brands), select Light Bulb, and paste the code. Once connected, you can assign your WiZ devices to rooms and control them through the SmartThings app.
